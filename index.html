FAQ

Is the XCSV bot ready?

Not yet. /mod is being built now and gets tested on a real community server before any client gets it. Tickets, live alerts and giveaways come after that.

The server build (channels, roles, onboarding, AutoMod) doesn't need the bot, and I can do it today.

Do I have to give the bot Administrator?

No. The XCSV bot asks only for the permissions each feature needs and never asks for Administrator.

For the build itself I need enough access to edit channels, roles and settings. We agree on that before I start, and you can remove my access once you've signed off.

I already use MEE6, Carl-bot or Sapphire. Do I have to drop them?

No. Keep them. The audit lists which bots overlap and what permissions they hold. While the XCSV bot is in development, I build around the bots you already use.

Is the audit really free? What do you need?

Yes. The fix list is yours whether you hire me or not. Email me your server's name, roughly how many members you have, what it's for, and what's bugging you. The email button fills in those questions for you. If I need to see inside the server, I'll say so.

Will you delete my channels?

I reorganize instead of deleting. On my last build every old channel kept its ID, so links and history still work. If something should go, I ask you first.

How long does a build take?

It depends on the size of the server and which build you pick. You get a date from me before you pay anything.

Who owns the server?

You do, before, during and after. I never ask for ownership, and everything I build stays in your server when the job is done.

What if I cancel the care plan?

Your server build stays exactly as it is. The plan only pays for ongoing work: updates, new features and support.

How does payment work?

Half up front, half on delivery. After handover you have 5 days to check everything and sign off. If something isn't right, tell me in those 5 days.

Is this just you?

Yes. XCSV is one person. I do the audit, the build and the support, and I'm the one who answers your email.
